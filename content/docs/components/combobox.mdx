## Notes

- **Use Select for short lists.** Reach for Combobox once a list needs search,
  roughly ten options or more, or when options carry a description.
- **Search matches label and keywords.** `keywords` adds terms that are not in
  the label, such as a country for a city.
- **Groups keep first-seen order.** Options sharing a `group` render under one
  heading, in the order their first option appears.
- **Clearing is opt-in.** With `clearable`, a clear button appears beside the
  chevron and picking the selected option again clears it.
- **Works in native forms.** Pass `name` and the value is submitted through a
  hidden input.
- **The trigger matches SelectTrigger**, including `size` and `mode`, so the two
  line up in a form.
